📋 How It Works

👤 Sign In

Choose your name from the list. Type your student number. Tap Sign In. Then you will see your plan.

🧪 Your Plan

Your plan shows your lessons. Do them in order. The star shows the lesson to do next. When you pass a lesson, the next one opens.

🎯 Show What You Know

A lesson starts with 5 questions. If you get all 5 right, you skip the lesson. You go on to the next one. If you miss one, that is OK. The lesson will teach you.

👀 Watch, 🪜 Step by Step, ✏️ Your Turn

First you watch and listen. Then you try a problem one step at a time. The steps do not count. Then it is your turn. Get 9 of 10 right to pass the lesson.

👂 Listen First

Every question has a Listen button. Tap it. Hear the question to the end. Then you can pick your answer. If you miss a question, tap Listen again. Hear what went wrong. Then the Next button shows.

⭐ Your Own Facts

Some questions have a star. A star means you missed that fact before. Take your time on it. If you get it right two times, it comes off your list.

🖐️ Use Your Fingers

You can count on your fingers. You can use the number line on the screen. You can use paper and a pencil. Ask Mr. O if you are stuck.

⏱ Overall Timer

A timer runs the whole time. It is not a countdown. There is no time limit. You can stop any time. Your work is saved. Next time you start where you stopped.
